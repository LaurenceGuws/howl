Howl Vulkan ABI provenance
==========================

`src/abi.zig` is maintained Zig source derived from
KhronosGroup/Vulkan-Headers tag v1.4.357, commit
e3b1eec08173d6b825cd3ac88c885a63b621504a. The authoritative
`include/vulkan/vulkan_core.h` input has SHA-256
a7ac0d7e35e77f642c175af5d36729cfdae2d626d2aaa9145c030a42bd44edc6.
Vulkan-Headers is licensed Apache-2.0 OR MIT. The maintained ABI preserves
that SPDX expression and the exact source revision.

Surface shaders
---------------

`src/shaders/*.spv` are the validated SPIR-V artifacts used by `src/surface.zig`;
their source inventory is chrome.vert, solid.frag, text.frag, and image.frag.
They are copied from the previously validated Howl shader set without changing
bytes during the surface-owner move. Validation remains part of the surface
package gate; no Host shader path is required at runtime.
