Exact required shape:

- Delete `Screen.saved_cursor`.
- Delete `screen_set.Set.CursorSnapshot`.
- Delete `screen_set.Set.saved_primary_cursor`.
- Add terminal-owned Kitty-like savepoints.
- Keep savepoint payload Kitty-like: cursor position, cursor shape including no-shape, blink or steady bit, pen or current display attrs, origin mode, auto-wrap mode, charset state, valid bit, and distinct savepoint per main and alt bank.
- Keep cursor visibility out of the savepoint payload.
- Keep cursor colors out of the savepoint payload.
- Keep public C ABI widening out of this slice.
- Keep render and host consumers untouched in this slice.

Slice invariants:

- Save and restore is terminal-owned, not screen-owned.
- Alt-screen cursor consequences follow Kitty semantics, not copied-primary semantics.
- No-shape is explicit VT shape truth, not collapsed hidden visibility.
- `47` switches only.
- `1047` clears alt on enter and does not save or restore.
- `1049` clears alt on enter and uses the Kitty-like savepoint.
