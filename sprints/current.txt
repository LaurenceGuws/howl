Hard doctrine:

- `howl-render` exposes exactly the host widget draw/render computation and state definitions. Nothing more. Nothing less.
- Public render ABI nouns and verbs must match host widget nouns and verbs exactly.
- `howl-render` internals may own exact computation nouns such as shaping, layout, rasterization, atlas packing, and damage computation.
- `howl-render` must not own host presentation policy, GL texture lifetime, window cadence, tab orchestration, scroll-bar orchestration, terminal orchestration, or host-side widget control.
- Use `presentation`, never `realization`.
- Use `trigger`, never `request` where a wake/action signal is meant.
- Use exact widget nouns: `term_surface`, `tab_bar_surface`, `scroll_bar_surface`.
- Do not use `scrollbar_surface`.
- Do not preserve generic host UI surface worldview: no `HostSurface`, `host_surface`, or god `Surface` hiding widget ownership.
- `src/term.zig` owns terminal-instance `term_surface` turns only.
- `src/tab_bar.zig` or `src/tab_bar/*` owns `tab_bar_surface` control and turns.
- `src/scroll_bar.zig` or `src/scroll_bar/*` owns `scroll_bar_surface` control and turns.
- Any control over tab-bar or scroll-bar surfaces must not persist in `src/buckets that must die/bucket2.zig` or `src/term.zig`.
