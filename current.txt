## Purpose

Extract pure render-surface submit diagnostic state, logging, and counter mutation from `terminal/context.zig` into an owner-true diagnostics file without moving trusted failure-policy or submit sequencing.

## Orchestration Rule

- If ownership, naming, scope, test gates, or ABI consequences are under-specified, stop and report orchestration needed.
- Do not silently fill design gaps, broaden scope, choose new owner names, or turn an ambiguous path into implementation.

## Allowed Files

- `howl-linux-host/src/terminal/context.zig`
- `howl-linux-host/src/terminal/render_surface_submit_diagnostics.zig`

No other files are in scope.

## Required Shape

- Create `terminal/render_surface_submit_diagnostics.zig` as the pure diagnostics owner.
- Move `Context.RenderSurfaceSubmitDiagnostics` from `context.zig` as `render_surface_submit_diagnostics.Diagnostics`.
- Keep this alias in `Context`: `pub const RenderSurfaceSubmitDiagnostics = render_surface_submit_diagnostics.Diagnostics;`.
- Move pure logging/counter helpers only. Do not move trusted failure-policy helpers or submit sequencing.
- The diagnostics owner must not import `terminal/context.zig`, accept `*Context`, accept `anytype self`, or back-reference the god aggregate.
- `Context.logRenderSurfaceDiagnostics()` remains a thin wrapper that computes `label = self.renderSurfaceLabel()` and passes plain inputs to diagnostics.
- `renderSurfaceLabel()` stays in `Context`.

## Required Mapping

- Every pure direct mutation of `render_surface_submit_diagnostics` in `ContextSubmitBackend.upload(...)`, `uploadRenderSurfaceCommands(...)`, `recordUnsupportedRenderSurfaceShape(...)`, and the first bucket switch in `recordRenderSurfaceUnavailable(...)` must become a diagnostics owner recording helper call.
- `recordUnsupportedRenderSurfaceShape(...)` stays in `context.zig` as a policy-adjacent wrapper that computes `term_texture.renderSurfaceSummary(render_surface)` and calls `recordUnsupportedShape(...)`.
- `recordRenderSurfaceUnavailable(...)` stays in `context.zig` because it enforces trusted failure policy. Its diagnostic bucket mutation must call `recordUnavailable(...)` before the policy switch.
- Status assignment in `ContextSubmitBackend.upload(...)` must call `recordEmitStatus(...)` and `recordResourcePlanStatus(...)`.
- Shape attempts/presents/failures in `uploadRenderSurfaceCommands(...)` must call `recordShape(...)`.
- `ShapeKind.glyph_patch` maps to existing glyph counters: `.surface` increments `render_surface_glyph_count`, `.present` increments `render_surface_glyph_present_count`, and `.failure` increments `render_surface_glyph_failure_count`.
