# Current Slice: VT Graphics Image-Number Identity

Owner: workspace root.

Sprint scope: graphics.

Promoted from `graphics-machinery-scratchpad.md` item 4 after image-number identity research.

## Goal

Match Kitty's observable image-number identity semantics without changing ABI: `I=` uploads allocate a free client image id without colliding with explicit `i=` images, while frame uploads by `I=` target the newest existing image instead of allocating a new one.

## Target Files

VT only:

- `howl-vt/src/kitty/graphics.zig`
- `howl-vt/src/test/terminal_graphics.zig`

## Required Behavior

- Keep `howl_vt.h`, render, and hosts unchanged.
- Preserve image publication ABI and payload-copy behavior.
- `a=t/T,I=<number>` with no `i=` allocates the lowest free positive client image id.
- `I=` allocation must not replace or delete an existing explicit `i=` image.
- Repeated `I=<number>` uploads create distinct client ids; future `I=<number>` references resolve to the newest matching image.
- Reusing a freed client id is allowed when it is the lowest free positive id.
- `a=f,I=<number>` stores the frame on the newest existing image with that number.
- Chunked `a=f,I=<number>` stores the frame on the same resolved existing image captured by the first chunk.
- Missing-image frame uploads by `I=` must not allocate a new image.
- Existing generated-placeholder, virtual-placement, quota, delete-selector, frame, and replacement behavior remains green.

## Hard Constraints

- Do not edit render or host.
- Do not edit `howl-vt/include/howl_vt.h`, `howl-vt/src/ffi.zig`, or `howl-vt/src/terminal.zig`.
- Do not introduce the full internal id/ref/cache rewrite.
- Do not change placement identity or render-facing publication structs.
- Do not solve anonymous `image_id == 0` multi-image identity in this slice.
- Do not special-case yazi/panes.
- Keep the smallest correct diff.

## Required Tests

- `I=` upload after existing `i=1` assigns a non-colliding free id and preserves `i=1`.
- Repeated `I=<number>` uploads assign distinct ids and placement by `I=<number>` targets the newest image.
- `I=` allocation reuses the lowest freed positive client id.
- Direct `a=f,I=<number>` stores a frame on the newest matching image.
- Chunked `a=f,I=<number>` stores a frame on the newest matching image captured by the first chunk.
- Missing `a=f,I=<number>` target replies not found and does not allocate an image.

## Verification Required

- `zig build test` in `howl-vt`
- `zig build test:regression:build` in `howl-vt`
- `git diff --check` at workspace root

Stop condition:

- If correct image-number behavior requires full internal image/ref identity or render ABI changes, stop and report the exact dependency instead of widening the slice.
