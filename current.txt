# Current Slice: Rename VT Surface Publication ABI

Owner: workspace root.

Purpose:

- Break the render C ABI to replace generic publish-slot vocabulary with VT-surface
  publication vocabulary.
- The ABI publishes VT surface source data into the render text session.
- No aliases, no compatibility shims, no old names.

- `HowlRenderVtPublishResult` -> `HowlRenderVtSurfacePublishResult`
