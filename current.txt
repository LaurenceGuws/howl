- `c` selects the current frame when present.
- `s` sets animation state when present.
- `v` sets loop count when present.
- Preserve existing success/failure reply behavior and quiet behavior.
- Do not special-case `icat`, `choose_files`, or any other client.
- Do not change parser, render, host, ABI, frame upload, compose, delete, runtime timing,
  storage identity, or generated-placeholder behavior.
