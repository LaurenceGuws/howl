- Render owns prepared render data and retained render state.
- Render does not own backend publication or presentation.

  present or publish to a backend.
- No compatibility aliases or shim files.
- Do not add `frame/abi.zig`, renamed ABI modules, `contract.zig`, generic
  `types.zig` outside the explicit `surface/types.zig` move, managers,
