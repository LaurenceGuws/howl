## Required Product Shape

- Add host-owned GL texture realization for accepted V0 resource operations:
  create texture, upload texture rect, retire texture.
- Invoke realization during `submitPreparedLocked()` after acquiring the prepared
  upload and before the unchanged full RGBA terminal texture upload.
- Persist bounded success/failure/accounting in retained render state.
- Invalid V0 resource realization must be accounting-only and must not affect the
  existing full RGBA upload/submit path.
- Do not draw V0 commands. Do not bind V0 resources into presentation.

- Existing prepared upload result and submit path remain full RGBA.
