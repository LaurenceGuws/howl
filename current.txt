# Current Slice: VT Graphics Frame Delete Promotion

Owner: workspace root.

Sprint scope: graphics.

Promoted from `graphics-machinery-scratchpad.md` item 5 after animation frame deletion research.

## Goal

Match Kitty's frame deletion semantics without changing ABI: deleting root frame promotes the first extra frame to root, deleting extra frames repairs frame numbering/current publication, and capital `d=F` frees the image when no extra frames remain.

## Target Files

VT only:

- `howl-vt/src/kitty/graphics.zig`
- `howl-vt/src/test/terminal_graphics.zig`

## Required Behavior

- Keep `howl_vt.h`, render, and hosts unchanged.
- Preserve image publication ABI and payload-copy behavior.
- `a=d,d=f,r=1` promotes frame `2` payload/metadata/gap into the root image and removes one extra frame.
- Deleting an extra frame removes it positionally, preserves frame order, and renumbers later frames.
- Missing or too-large `r=` normalizes to Kitty's target frame instead of no-oping unexpectedly.
- Deleting the current frame or a prior frame repairs `current_frame_number` and refreshes the published current payload.
- `a=d,d=f` with no extra frames keeps the image alive.
- `a=d,d=F` with no extra frames frees the image.
- Existing generated-placeholder, virtual-placement, quota, delete-selector, image-number, frame loading, frame compose, placement-kind replacement, and stable-parent-ref behavior remains green.

## Hard Constraints

- Do not edit render or host.
- Do not edit `howl-vt/include/howl_vt.h`, `howl-vt/src/ffi.zig`, or `howl-vt/src/terminal.zig`.
- Do not introduce the full internal id/ref/cache rewrite or persistent generated cell refs.
- Do not change placement identity or render-facing publication structs.
- Do not solve anonymous `image_id == 0` multi-image identity in this slice.
- Do not replace retained base64 payload truth with decoded/cache storage in this slice.
- Do not change animation runtime timing or visibility policy.
- Do not special-case yazi/panes.
- Keep the smallest correct diff.

## Required Tests

- Deleting root frame promotes frame `2` into root, removes one extra frame, shifts old frame `3` to frame `2`, and repairs current frame to root when needed.
- Deleting a middle extra frame preserves remaining frame order, renumbers later frames, and repairs a selected later current frame.
- `a=d,d=f` with no `r=` repeatedly promotes until no extra frames remain, then leaves the final root image alive.
- `a=d,d=F` with no extra frames deletes the image and associated frame state.
- Missing or too-large `r=` deletes Kitty's normalized target frame.
- Existing frame compose/current-frame publication tests remain green.
