# Current Slice: Kitty Quiet Response Modes

Owner: workspace root.

Sprint scope: graphics.

Promoted from `graphics-machinery-scratchpad.md` after protocol-matrix research.

## Goal

Preserve Kitty's distinct graphics response suppression modes: `q=1` suppresses successful `OK` responses, while `q=2` suppresses failure responses.

## Target Files

- `howl-vt/src/kitty/graphics.zig`
- `howl-vt/src/action/vocabulary.zig`
- `howl-vt/src/kitty/protocol.zig`
- `howl-vt/src/test/action_mapping.zig`
- `howl-vt/src/test/terminal_graphics.zig`

## Required Behavior

- Parser/action vocabulary preserves the numeric quiet mode instead of collapsing nonzero values to `true`.
- `q=0` and omitted `q` keep current unsuppressed reply behavior.
- `q=1` suppresses successful `OK` replies but still emits failures.
- `q=2` suppresses failures but still emits successful `OK` replies.
- Chunked uploads carry the initiating quiet mode through final success/error handling.
- Placement, query/transmit, animation/frame, compose, parent, and validation reply sites use success-vs-error suppression rather than a boolean.

## Hard Constraints

- Do not touch ABI, render, host, or terminal files.
- Do not edit `howl-vt/src/terminal.zig`.
- Do not expand into PNG `EBADPNG` taxonomy.
- Do not change image decode/storage semantics.
- Do not alter delete/reset behavior, animation runtime, frame gaps, or placement geometry.
- Do not add compatibility aliases that blur `q=1` and `q=2`.
- Do not special-case yazi/panes.
- Keep the smallest correct diff.

## Required Tests

- Parser/action test proves `q=0`, `q=1`, and `q=2` remain distinct.
- Invalid direct upload with `q=1` still emits an error.
- Invalid direct upload with `q=2` emits no error.
- Successful direct upload/query/placement with `q=1` emits no `OK`.
- Successful direct upload/query/placement with `q=2` still emits `OK`.
- Failed chunked upload with initiating `q=1` emits final error.
- Failed chunked upload with initiating `q=2` emits no final error.
- Missing image/frame/parent errors with `q=2` emit no error.
- Successful animation/frame control with `q=1` emits no `OK`.

## Verification Required

- `zig build test` in `howl-vt`
- `zig build test:regression:build` in `howl-vt`
- `git diff --check` at workspace root

Stop condition:

- If correct suppression requires broad reply taxonomy changes beyond success/error helpers, stop and report the exact dependency instead of widening the slice.
