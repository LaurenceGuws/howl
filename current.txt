Owner: workspace root.

Purpose:

## Current Source Facts

- `howl-linux-host/src/main.zig` currently defines `FramePacingState` inside the app/event-loop owner.
- `App.frame_pacing` stores that state directly.
- `computeLoopAdmission`, `runLoopTurn`, and `submitPresent` call pacing methods.
- `PresentReason` is currently in `main.zig` and is consumed by `presentSubmissionPermission`.
- `loopWaitMs` already accepts a `frame_pacer_wait_ms` hook, but callers pass `null`.
- A real frame deadline requires a scheduler owner and post-swap scheduling, so it is out of scope.

## Invariants

- `present_complete_pending` implies `present_in_flight`.
- `frame_permit_ready == false` after a submitted present.
- `frame_permit_ready == true` after present completion.
- `present_in_flight == false` after present completion.
- `renderPermission()` is false when `frame_permit_ready == false`.
- Redraw/render work records intent, not frame permission.
- Runtime wake prevents blocking, but does not grant render permission.
- `.none` and `.terminal_retire` are never submit-permitted.
- `.host_damage` and `.terminal_frame` require frame permit and no in-flight present.
- `noteRenderSubmitted()` clears `redraw_requested`, matching current behavior.
