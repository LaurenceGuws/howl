## Invariants

- Host owns blink cadence; VT owns cursor visible/blink truth.
- `State.resetActivity(now_ns)` sets the next deadline to `now_ns + interval` and makes blink visible.
- Non-animated cursor returns no wait and forces visible state.
- Animated cursor with no deadline starts a deadline without toggling visibility.
- Expired deadlines toggle visibility and advance by fixed intervals until after `now_ns`.
- Context remains responsible for applying render cursor blink visibility through render C ABI.
