# Current Slice: Display-Owned Render-Surface Upload Policy

Active research:

- `research/cache-2026-06-02-context-submit-boundary.md`

Reviewer acceptance:

- Move render-surface upload dispatch and patch/full upload policy out of `ContextSubmitBackend` into `display/renderer/render_surface.zig`.
- Add exact display entrypoint: `pub fn uploadRenderSurface(textures: *RenderResourceTextures, host_surface: *render_c.HowlRenderHostSurface, surface: *const render_c.HowlRenderSurface) bool`.

## Problem

After render-surface contract validation moved into `display/renderer/render_surface_contract.zig`, `ContextSubmitBackend` still owns display-renderer policy: render-surface shape dispatch, patch/full upload policy, unsupported-shape panic, and upload failure cleanup. Context should own the host turn and submit transaction. Display renderer should own render-surface upload classification, host surface realization, GL upload/draw execution, and patch/full policy.

## Required Shape

In `display/renderer/render_surface.zig`:

- Add `pub fn uploadRenderSurface(textures: *RenderResourceTextures, host_surface: *render_c.HowlRenderHostSurface, surface: *const render_c.HowlRenderSurface) bool`.
- Move shape dispatch currently in `ContextSubmitBackend.uploadRenderSurfaceCommands`.
- Move patch/full upload policy currently in `ContextSubmitBackend.renderSurfaceUploadPolicy`.
- Move `RenderSurfaceUploadPolicyError`.
- Move `crashOnRenderSurfaceUploadPolicyError`.
- Move `panicUnsupportedTrustedRenderSurfaceShape`.
- `uploadRenderSurface` computes matching-host-surface policy from `host_surface` and `surface.render_px`.
- `uploadRenderSurface` calls `textures.realizeSurface(surface)`.
- `uploadRenderSurface` calls `ensureSurface(host_surface, surface.render_px.width, surface.render_px.height)`.
- `uploadRenderSurface` dispatches to existing upload functions.
- On upload failure, `uploadRenderSurface` must zero `host_surface.width` and `host_surface.height` to preserve current context behavior.
- Keep GL texture ownership, `RenderResourceTextures`, rollback, host surface texture, GL failures, upload/draw execution, and shape classifiers in `render_surface.zig`.

In `terminal/context.zig`:

- `ContextSubmitBackend.upload` handles null `prepared_upload.render_surface` only through retrieval-status panic mapping.
- Non-null render surface delegates to `term_texture.uploadRenderSurface(&self.render_surface_textures, &self.term_texture, surface)`.
- Delete `ContextSubmitBackend.uploadRenderSurfaceCommands`.
- Delete `ContextSubmitBackend.renderSurfaceUploadPolicy`.
- Delete `ContextSubmitBackend.RenderSurfaceUploadPolicyError`.
- Delete `ContextSubmitBackend.crashOnRenderSurfaceUploadPolicyError`.
- Delete `ContextSubmitBackend.panicUnsupportedTrustedRenderSurfaceShape`.
- Delete `ContextSubmitBackend.shouldRealizeRenderSurface` if it no longer has a true context-owned role.
- Keep retrieval-status panic mapping in context: `RenderSurfaceRetrievalError`, `renderSurfaceRetrievalError`, `unavailableRenderSurfaceRetrievalError`, `crashOnRenderSurfaceRetrievalStatus`.
- Keep `ContextSubmitBackend.execution`.
- Keep context-owned host surface field assignment after successful submit.
- Do not change submit transaction lock/unlock/relock behavior.
