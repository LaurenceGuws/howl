# Current Slice: VT Graphics Quota Eviction

Owner: workspace root.

Sprint scope: graphics.

Promoted from `graphics-machinery-scratchpad.md` item 4 after identity/quota research was recorded in root `0095a75`.

## Goal

Add ABI-preserving VT-owned Kitty graphics quota eviction for safe candidates before retained-payload limits fail uploads.

## Target Files

VT only:

- `howl-vt/src/kitty/graphics.zig`
- `howl-vt/src/test/terminal_graphics.zig`

## Required Behavior

- Keep `howl_vt.h`, render, and hosts unchanged.
- Keep public `image_id`, `image_number`, `placement_id`, publication order, and payload-copy behavior unchanged.
- Before returning retained-payload `ConsequenceLimit`, evict safe candidates where possible.
- First-slice eviction order is conservative: incomplete/aborted upload state if applicable, then unplaced images and their frames/current-frame override payloads.
- Do not evict images that have physical placements or virtual placements in this slice.
- Replacement of an existing `image_id` must count bytes freed by the replacement before failing quota.
- Evicting an image must remove its frames and any current-frame/override payload state.
- Existing image-number newest lookup, replacement, delete selector, generated-placeholder, and publication behavior must remain green.

## Hard Constraints

- Do not edit render or host.
- Do not edit `howl-vt/include/howl_vt.h`, `howl-vt/src/ffi.zig`, or `howl-vt/src/terminal.zig` unless a compile blocker proves otherwise.
- Do not introduce the full internal id/ref/cache rewrite in this slice.
- Do not treat base64 transport payload as the desired long-term product model, but keep the current payload-copy ABI unchanged for this slice.
- Do not evict images with physical or virtual placements yet.
- Do not special-case yazi/panes.
- Keep the smallest correct diff.

## Required Tests

- Quota evicts an unplaced image before failing or touching a placed image.
- Quota preserves placed images and returns `ConsequenceLimit` when only placed images could make room.
- Replacement of the same `image_id` succeeds when it fits after subtracting bytes freed by the old image.
- Evicting an unplaced image also removes its frames and current-frame/override payload state.

## Verification Required

- `zig build test` in `howl-vt`
- `zig build test:regression:build` in `howl-vt`
- `git diff --check` at workspace root

Stop condition:

- If implementing quota safely requires changing the public graphics ABI or evicting placed images, stop and report the exact dependency instead of widening the slice.
