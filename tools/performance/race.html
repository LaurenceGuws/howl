One bound across the SDL handoff

SDL 3.4.18 merges adjacent same-texture geometry calls. Its OpenGL backend expands each quad to six 32-byte vertices; a nearly full text grid passes 4 MiB. On NVIDIA 615.71.09, sparse growth produces many distinct retained device mappings above that size. Submitting each 2048-quad batch prevents the merge from widening the draw. No renderer replacement, extra cache or GPU-completion wait is added.

Decision and evidence · 14 ordinary folds

Shared text owner under contention

Eight native 1/2/4-pane runs at 60/240 Hz: 19 producers, 39,600 frames and zero skipped slots. Four rich panes then force a shared 256-entry glyph store through a 384-glyph union while each local atlas stays within its bound. Reviewed final captures contain no failure panel.

One process completed three 1→2→4→2→1 pane cycles and 18→19→18 font-size transitions. The regular font mapping count follows 1→2→1; equal-size panes keep one mapping per configured style. Nineteen quiet cuts record zero root CPU ticks at sampler resolution. One-pane anonymous storage remains about 10 MiB above the first cut after three cycles; retained pools have not been called leak-free.
