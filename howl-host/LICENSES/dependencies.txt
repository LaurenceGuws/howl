howl-host links the installed system Wayland client, wayland-egl, EGL, GLESv2,
and xkbcommon libraries under their installed licenses.

howl-host selects howl-render's native-text capability, which links the
installed system FreeType and HarfBuzz libraries. Font test assets and their
provenance remain owned by howl-render.
